The benchmark includes MI4, MI2, P300, ERP and SSVEP. P300 uses eight Hoffmann
participants/four sessions, target/non-target epochs, not our OpenBMI character
allocation. Nine defense approaches plus normal training are evaluated with
three CNNs. Within-session uses first80% training; cross-session uses first
session training and remaining sessions testing. Offline alignment uses training
and unlabeled testing EEG; online alignment uses training only. Five repeats
are reported. Primary metrics are balanced classification accuracy and targeted
attack success, not clean-calibrated character rejection or accepted-error cost.

This is substantial prior P300 robustness/cross-session evidence, not merely
an MI or abstract-only precedent. Do not claim first P300 benchmark, first
shift analysis or superior generalization from our different protocol. Our
character decision unit, clean-only calibration and all-attempt rejection-cost
estimand differ; informative independent findings are still necessary.
No frozen methods, thresholds, allocations or endpoints changed after this review.
